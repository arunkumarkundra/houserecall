    How HouseRecall Works

    Simple, private, and totally in your hands.

    🧠

    Your personal memory upgrade

    HouseRecall is dead simple: add rooms, add stuff, find stuff instantly. No tutorials required — if you've ever used a sticky note, you're already an expert.

    🔒

    Privacy-first, always

    Your data lives entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server, ever. No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking pixels, no cookies following you around the internet. It's your home — it should stay private.

    🚫

    No login. Seriously.

    We don't have a login page. We don't have a server to log into. There's no "Forgot password?" because there's no password to forget. Open the page, start using it — that's it.

    💾

    Your data, full control

    Everything is stored locally using your browser's IndexedDB — it persists between sessions automatically. Export your entire house to a .house file any time. Import it on another device. You own the file, you own the data.

    🔐

    Military-grade encryption (no exaggeration)

    When you export, your data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password you choose. The password never leaves your browser. Even if someone finds your .house file, it's unreadable without your password.

    ✈️

    Works offline, always

    No internet connection needed — ever. HouseRecall runs entirely in your browser with zero cloud dependency. Use it on a plane, in a basement, on a desert island. As long as your browser works, it works.

    🎒

    Take it anywhere

    Export your .house file and import it on any device — phone, laptop, tablet. Handy when you're at one home and need to remember what's stored at another.
