Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025 · Effective immediately

🎯 TL;DR — The honest two-liner

HouseRecall collects absolutely nothing about you. Your data lives in your browser. We have no server, no database, no account system, and no analytics. We literally cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

👤 Who we are

HouseRecall is a browser-based personal organisation tool. There is no company server, no backend infrastructure, and no data pipeline. The "we" in this policy refers to the creators of this software — not a data controller in the traditional sense, because we control nothing.

📊 What data we collect

None. Zero. Zip. Nada.

HouseRecall runs entirely in your browser using:

  • IndexedDB — your locations and items are stored locally in your browser's built-in database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
  • Web Crypto API — used locally to encrypt your export files. Your encryption password is never transmitted anywhere.
  • No cookies — we set zero cookies, tracking or otherwise.
  • No analytics — no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no pixel trackers, nothing.
  • No external requests — other than loading the Google Fonts stylesheet (see below), the app makes no network requests at runtime.

🖋️ Google Fonts

HouseRecall loads two fonts (Fraunces and DM Sans) from Google Fonts via a CSS import. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to download those font files. Google may log your IP address as part of that request.

If this concerns you, you can self-host the fonts or remove the import from styles.css — the app will fall back to system fonts gracefully.

💾 Your exported .house files

When you export your data, a .house file is downloaded to your device. This file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password you choose. We never see this file, never receive it, and have no way to decrypt it — your password exists only in your head and your browser session.

You are fully responsible for the security of your exported files. Store them somewhere safe, and don't forget your password — there is no recovery mechanism because there is no server to recover it from.

🗑️ Deleting your data

Your data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. To delete it completely:

  • Clear your browser's site data for this domain (Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Site data)
  • Or use your browser's developer tools to delete the houserecall IndexedDB database

There is no "delete my account" button because there is no account. Once you clear the browser data, it's gone permanently.

👶 Children's privacy

HouseRecall does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, this is a non-issue in practice — but we mention it for completeness.

🔄 Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy (for example, if we add analytics or a server component), we will update the "last updated" date above and make it prominent. We don't anticipate this happening — the privacy-first, local-only architecture is a core design principle of HouseRecall, not an afterthought.

✉️ Contact

Questions, concerns, or just want to say hi? Since there's no data to ask about, there's not much to contact us about — but if you find a bug or have a feature idea, feel free to reach out via the project's repository.
